# AGENT.md

Entry for make.fun competition "The Best Advice I Ever Got"
(u-pangg7-the-best-advice-i-ever-got-358cefb0).

The entry is one sentence (179 characters) about the best advice the writer
ever received. It names the giver (Grandma) and a real moment (shelling peas
on the porch the night Dad left), and the advice itself is concrete and
earned: marry the one who washes the dishes without being asked, because love
is what people do when no one is watching. No generic aphorism — the judge's
brief calls a wise-sounding line with no person behind it filler.

`main.py` (stdlib only) writes the sentence to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` with no trailing newline.
